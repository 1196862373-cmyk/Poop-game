# Splat Poop at Your Boss and Annoying People 🚀

An ultimate web mini-game built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to help you relax and destress instantly by launching objects at your targets!

## 🎮 How to Play
1. ⚡ **Cyber Wooden Fish Mode**: 
   - Click the fish to generate crisp "Dong" sounds and stack up your spiritual blessings!
2. 💥 **Full-Angle Particle Bombardment**: 
   - Upload a photo of your boss, an annoying person, or any funny emoji.
   - Click anywhere on the photo! Giant poops (💩) will fly in from random angles with realistic custom sound effects ("Whoosh... Splat!") and stick precisely to their faces.
3. 🎉 **The Grand Finale**: 
   - Successfully land 20 hits to trigger the ultimate ending—the target photo will explode and transform into a giant, shaking emoji!

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **HTML5**: Page structure and components.
- **CSS3**: Cyberpunk dark mode UI and explosion particle animations.
- **JavaScript**: Core logic, sound wave synthesizer, and motion path equations.
